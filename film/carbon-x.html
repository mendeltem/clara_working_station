Carbon-X explained: an AI model that reads DNA

An 8-minute film on Carbon-X (Hugging Face Bio, Apache 2.0), installed and run locally on the working station with llama.cpp. English, 1080p. Built by hand with the same film tool, not by Clara. No patient data in it: the numbers come from the authors' release blog and model card, plus a small own test on public ClinVar variants. Carbon-X is not clinically validated.
